# Test run (fictional business only)

**Status:** Worked example for internal review. The business, town, items, and prices below are invented. Do not use real shop names, addresses, staff names, customer text, or private data.

---

## Fictional setup (as if getting-started were already done)

| Memory | Value |
|---|---|
| Business | Sunny Corner Bakery — a neighborhood bakery-cafe |
| Location | Maplebrook (fictional town) |
| Languages | Chinese and English, as separate drafts |
| Platforms | Google Business Profile, Instagram, Facebook, X, WeChat Moments, 小红书, Yelp update, delivery-app blurb |
| Tone | Warm, casual, neighborly. Light emoji OK. Google: no hashtags. Instagram / 小红书: 3–8 tags. X: at most one tag. |
| Banned | “Best bakery in town,” “#1,” competitor names, invented awards, discounts not in the source |
| Hours note | Closed Mondays |
| Approval | Draft only; wait for the shop owner to approve each post |
| Frequency | About 4 posts a week |

---

## Input (from the shop owner)

> We're running a weekend slice: strawberry cream cake, Saturday and Sunday only, $8.50 a slice. The photo is a close-up of a slice — golden sponge, whipped cream, and fresh strawberries on top. I want regulars to know, and I want something I can also put on Google and Instagram. Closed Mondays. Don't say “best bakery in town.” Casual, friendly, not shouty.

The bot does not invent an address, phone number, certification, or extra discount. It uses only this note plus the saved memories above.

## What the bot correctly refused to add

- No street address, phone number, or map pin (not in the source).
- No “best bakery in town” or similar ranking language (banned + not in the source).
- No extra discount, combo, or “while supplies last” urgency (not in the source).
- No review quotes or customer names.

## Optional follow-up the bot might ask (one at a time, only if useful)

1. Do you also want this on a story/Reel sticker, or just the main posts above?
2. Any photo credit or in-store chalkboard line you want to keep consistent?

After this, the bot stops and waits for the shop owner to approve, edit, or drop each draft.
