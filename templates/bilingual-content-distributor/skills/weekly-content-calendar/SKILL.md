# Weekly content calendar

Draft a one-week content plan the owner can approve, change, or ignore. This is a proposal, not a schedule, and not permission to publish.

## What to use

- Saved memories: business type, languages, platforms, tone, banned words, posting frequency, closed days.
- Anything the owner already told you about this week (specials, new items, hours changes, events).
- Upcoming holidays or local events in the next 2–3 weeks, only when relevant to this kind of business. If you are unsure a holiday applies (for example a religious observance), list it as an optional idea and ask, do not assume.

## Plan shape

Propose only as many slots as the owner's frequency allows. A light default for a busy shop: 3–5 posts a week, not one on every platform every day.

For each slot, give:

- Day and a suggested time-of-day window (for example “weekday morning” or “Friday late afternoon”)
- Theme
- Platforms (not all platforms every day)
- Language(s)
- One-line angle the owner can shoot or write from
- What you still need from them (usually a photo or a confirmed price)

Also add a short “coming up” note for holidays or events in the next 2–3 weeks, and 2–3 backup ideas if a photo falls through.

## Theme mix (rotate; do not repeat the same promo all week)

- New item or limited special
- Everyday signature / “what to try if it is your first visit”
- Behind the scenes or people (only with the owner's permission)
- Useful update: hours, holiday schedule, how to order or book
- Community or local seasonal hook
- Light educational or cultural note, if it fits the business and stays factual

Reuse one idea across two platforms at most, and rewrite it each time. Leave at least one open slot for a last-minute special.

## Output format

A compact table or list (Day | Theme | Platforms | Language | Angle | Needed from owner), then the “coming up” note and backup ideas. Ask the owner: “Which slots should I turn into full drafts now?” Wait for the answer. Do not write every post automatically unless they ask.
