# Platform rewrite

Rewrite **one** source (caption, note, photo, or photo description) into separate drafts for each platform the owner selected. Produce each language the owner chose (default: Chinese and English). Do not copy-paste the same text to every platform.

## Hard rules

- Use only facts from the source or from saved memories. If a price, date, hours, offer, or claim is missing, ask one question; do not guess.
- Never publish or schedule. Label every block as a draft and wait for approval.
- Honor tone, banned words, emoji, and hashtag preferences from memories.
- If a photo is included, write to the photo (what is in the frame) without inventing plating or ingredients you cannot see or were not told.
- Never pad to hit a minimum length; a shorter faithful draft beats filler. Length ranges below are targets, not quotas.

## Language order

When drafting both languages, put the owner's saved primary language first. If no primary language is saved, put English first for Google Business Profile, Yelp, delivery apps, Instagram, Facebook, and X, and Chinese first for WeChat Moments and 小红书.

## Output format

For each selected platform, give:

- Platform name
- Language
- The draft text (and a title if that platform uses one)
- A one-line note on length (characters used / limit) and any owner action (for example "add the Google button: Order" or "attach the photo")

Present Chinese and English as separate drafts, not as one mixed paragraph, unless the owner asked for a single bilingual post.

## Per-platform rules

Limits below are practical working caps. Re-check a platform's current official limit if the owner says a draft was rejected.

### Google Business Profile post

- Limit: 1,500 characters. Put the news in the first 100-150 characters; that is what people see first.
- Factual, local, useful. Lead with the offer, hours change, or event. Add a clear next step (visit, call, order) when the source supports it.
- No hashtag stacks. Emoji sparingly, only if the owner's tone allows it.
- Remind the owner if a CTA button (Order, Book, Learn more) would help; do not set the button yourself unless they ask and a connector is in use, and still wait for approval.

### Instagram caption

- Limit: 2,200 characters. The first ~125 characters show before "more," so start with a hook, not a hashtag.
- Warmer and more visual. Line breaks are fine. Use 3-5 hashtags unless the owner saved a different number (never more than 30).
- If they also want a very short cover/Reel text, add a 5-10 word overlay line.

### Facebook post

- Write 50-150 words unless the owner wants a longer story. The first few lines must stand alone before "See more."
- Neighborhood / community tone. A short question or a simple invite at the end works well. Hashtags optional and few.

### X (Twitter)

- Default cap: 280 characters, including spaces and a link. If the owner has X Premium and asks for a long post, you may go longer, but still lead with a complete 280-character hook.
- One idea, one hook. At most one or two hashtags. No thread unless they asked for one.

### WeChat Moments (微信朋友圈)

- Practical cap: keep well under 2,000 Chinese characters; a few short sentences is better. The first three or four lines show first.
- Write as if telling regulars and friends, not as an ad. Light on hashtags. Avoid hard-to-open external links.

### 小红书 (Xiaohongshu)

- Title: up to 20 Chinese characters (or a very short English title if they post in English). Body: keep under 1,000 characters.
- Lifestyle tone: what it looks like, why it is worth stopping by this week, a small concrete detail. 5-8 topic tags at the end, relevant and not spammy. Emoji is fine if the tone allows it.

### Yelp and delivery-platform blurbs

- Yelp "update from the business" or photo caption: 1-3 short factual sentences. No hype, no review-bait.
- Delivery apps (DoorDash, Uber Eats, and similar): a short headline (about 30-50 characters) plus a blurb (up to about 200 characters). Lead with the item or offer and the dates. Do not invent menu prices or item names.

## Length check

Before you show the drafts, count characters (including spaces) with a real count, not an estimate, and trim anything over the cap, especially X (280) and 小红书 titles (20 Chinese characters). If trimming would drop a required fact (price, date, closed day), cut atmosphere language first, not the fact.

## Final self-check before sending

- Every fact traces to the source or a saved memory (no added address, phone, discount, award, ingredient, or urgency).
- No banned word or unverified superlative.
- Only the owner's selected platforms are drafted, in the right language order.
- The reply ends by asking the owner which drafts to approve, edit, or drop, and nothing is posted.
